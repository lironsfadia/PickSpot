## Limits and unresolved items

- The image does not identify private, assigned or otherwise non-bookable desks.
  The user was asked to confirm eligibility. Existing booking policy is retained
  pending that answer; 108 is NOT a confirmed bookable-seat capacity.
- The dark-blue enclosed rooms, meeting tables, kitchen/counter stools and
  auditorium chairs are excluded from this desk inventory. Whether any should
  be separately bookable needs confirmation.
- Chair centres and desk angles are approximate traces of a low-resolution raster.
  In particular, West desk 10 and partially overlaid room boundaries merit
  confirmation against a higher-resolution plan or a facilities walkthrough.
- The approved illustrated background is decorative and differs from the source
  drawing. Current seat coordinates and overlaid furniture use reference space;
  a visual similarity to the background cannot independently verify geometry.
- Labels are editable. The existing seeding logic matches standard labels;
  renamed seats need separate review rather than being assumed to be verified.
